About Keith

Rooted in realism,
not political nostalgia

For too long, the livability of our city has been neglected in favor of decisions driven by short-term optics rather than long-term stability. I believe the future of Langford should be defined by how we can best serve the people living here today and tomorrow.

I know that every dollar the city spends comes from your hard-earned income. Instead of choosing short-term fixes that just hide the costs for later, I focus on the hard work of creating a stable foundation for city that is built to last.
