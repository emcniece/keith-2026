Achievements

Fiscal Accountability

Making every dollar count

By cutting wasteful spending, demanding transparency, and negotiating better contracts, we’ve improved essential services, amenities and infrastructure. We have ended hidden debts and updated development fees to ensure the city, not just developers, benefits.

Read More
Responsible Development

Growing our city the right way

We’re planning for the big picture instead of making piecemeal decisions. We’ve supported more rental housing, are creating more efficient transportation networks, and set higher energy standards for homes. We’ve also invested in new medical clinics to ensure families get the local care they need.

Public Engagement & Transparency

Putting you at the center of city decisions

Through local events and better feedback tools, we’ve ensured your voice shapes our plans. We’re building trust through new partnerships, clearer reports, and better access to development information, making local government work for everyone.

July 13, 2026 Read More
Green Space for All

Ensuring everyone has access to nature

We’ve protected over 200 acres of green spaces, secured critical park land in Langford’s downtown core, and planned for better park distribution. We launched our first community garden, and are creating a long-term plan to maintain our city’s natural environment for future generations.
